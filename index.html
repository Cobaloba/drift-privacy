Drift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Drift is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains exactly what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is short because the game collects very little.

Drift never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise location, and has no account system.

What is collected

WhatWhyWho handles it
Advertising data — device and advertising identifiers, IP address (which implies an approximate, city-level location), and whether you viewed or interacted with an ad. To show ads, which is how the game is paid for, and to stop showing you the same ad repeatedly. Google AdMob, under Google's privacy policy.
Gameplay analytics — a random identifier generated on your device, plus events such as which level you started, finished or left, how many attempts a level took, how long a flight lasted, whether you chose to watch an ad to skip a level, your platform and the app version. To find out which levels are unfair or frustrating, and whether ads appear too often. This is the only way to tell a hard level from a broken one. Sent to a private endpoint operated by the developer and stored there.
Your progress — levels beaten, stars collected, attempts per level. So the game remembers where you were. Stored only on your device. It is never uploaded, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or use "reset progress" in the level menu.

The random analytics identifier is not linked to you, your Google account, or your advertising ID. It exists so that ten sessions from one player can be told apart from one session by ten players. It is stored with your save data and disappears when the game is uninstalled.

Consent

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, Drift asks for your consent before it requests any ad or sends any analytics. That prompt is Google's standard consent form, and it appears after you have played rather than the moment you open the game.

If you decline, nothing is sent. No ads are requested and no analytics leave your device — the game plays exactly the same, without ads. You can change your answer at any time from the privacy option in the level menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings, under Google → Ads.

How long it is kept

Gameplay analytics are kept for at most 12 months and then deleted. Ad data is retained by Google according to their own policy, linked above.

Children

Drift is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact the address below and it will be deleted.

Selling data

Your data is not sold. Nothing is shared with anyone beyond Google AdMob, which serves the ads, and the developer's own analytics endpoint described above.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent at any time in the game, reset your advertising identifier in your device settings, or delete everything by uninstalling the game. To ask what has been stored against your device, or to have it deleted, contact the address below — please include roughly when you played, as there is no account to look you up by.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the app's store listing.
