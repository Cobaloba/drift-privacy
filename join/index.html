drift.

A gravity-slingshot puzzle. One shot. Bend it around the planets.

You'll need an Android phone and a Google account (the one signed in to the Play Store). The beta is open in the UK and Ireland.

  1. Join the tester group

    Sign in with the same Google account your Play Store uses, then tap Join group.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester on the Google Play page that opens.

    Become a tester

  3. Install Drift

    Opens the Play Store. Install like any other app.

    Get it on Google Play
Something not working?
  • No "Join group" button? You're not signed in. Sign in at the top of the page and it appears.
  • Play says the app isn't available? Access can take a few minutes to arrive after step 2. Wait a moment and try step 3 again.
  • More than one Google account on your phone? In the Play Store, tap your profile picture and switch to the account you joined with.
  • "Become a tester" says you're not eligible? Step 1 hasn't gone through yet, or you joined with a different account.

Thanks for playing! Please open it a few times over the next two weeks, because that helps Drift get to a full launch. Got stuck, found a bug, or loved a level?
